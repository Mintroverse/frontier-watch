Short answer: still the right bet — the standard we waited for shipped as promised, and the operators winning with AI are winning on discipline, not spend.

This week resolved the briefing's longest-running watch: the connector standard is final, matching its preview, with a formal deprecation guarantee — so the queued integration work proceeds on official rails.
